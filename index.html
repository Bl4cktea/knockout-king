How to win: a glove glowing on one side means a hook is coming, so dodge to the opposite side. A glove raised high means a jab: block it or sidestep. Punch only when his gloves drop. Guarded punches bounce off. Land hits in openings to earn stars, then spend one for a big stunning punch. Knock him down twice.
